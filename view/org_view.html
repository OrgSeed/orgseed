Structure: modules

Any combination may be on. Presets are shortcuts, not levels.

ModuleStateForAdopted by
charteroffTurning the founder's goal into a charter the members can work from: the goal and its reason, a few near-term goals, what success looks like, and the roles the founder's own view of organizing suggests.
decision-rightsoffMaking clear what each member may decide alone, what is reserved to people, what the founder has delegated and with what limits, and which decisions are waiting.
claim-disciplineoffMaking every factual claim the organization states traceable to a source someone has opened, and keeping the organization's own inferences visibly apart from what its sources say.
memoryoffCarrying what the organization knows from one session to the next, when its AI members carry nothing themselves, and when the next member may be a different model.
record-keepingoffA fuller journal of what the organization does: an event for every kind of activity, not only the ones the core asks for.
reviewoffAssigning checks to roles, so that a check is met whatever the member who holds it brings from its producer; keeping the one who made a piece of work apart from the one who checks it; and checking the checkers.
tool-makingoffLetting the organization gain tools in the same way in every organization grown from this seed: each step happens where the founder can see it, and the rules tighten the further a tool can reach.
membershipoffDeciding which AI members the organization admits, to which roles, from which producers; giving a role several instances, possibly on different models; and handling the moment a model in a slot changes.
normsoffThe way members work together beyond the formal rules: norms the founder adopts, each carried with its reason, a way to dissent, and the organization's own list of failure patterns it has learned to watch for.
engagementoffKeeping the founder engaged as a member of the organization, not as a supervisor reviewing its output: every session moves the goal, shows what the organization's standards turned back, and hands the founder a decision only they can make.
boundariesoffDeciding what the organization keeps, what never leaves it, which outside channels may receive what, and how the use of AI in its work is disclosed.
design-aheadoffLetting the founder set structure before the first incident rather than after it. Organizations often learn their rules from failure. A founder who knows how organizations fail can choose some of that structure in advance.
